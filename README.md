# My Favorite Movie: Four Sisters Before the Wedding

**Title:** Four Sisters Before the Wedding

**Genre:** Comedy, Drama, Family

**Release Year:** 2020

## Main Characters
- Teddie Salazar
- Bobbie Salazar
- Alex Salazar
- Gabbie Salazar

## Movie Description
*Four Sisters Before the Wedding* is a Filipino family drama-comedy movie about four sisters and their family experiences before their eldest sister's wedding.

## Why I Love This Movie
- It shows the importance of family.
- It teaches us to forgive and understand one another.
- It shows that siblings may fight but still love each other.
- It reminds us that family is always important.

## My Favorite Lesson
**Family is everything.** No matter how many misunderstandings and arguments happen, we should always learn to forgive, support, and love one another.

**10/10**
